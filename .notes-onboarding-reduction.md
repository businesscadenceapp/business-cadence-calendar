## Scope note: sandbox preview
Notes above document the deliberate design choice: static sample-data preview carousel on
WaitingForPartner instead of a full in-app sandbox flag. Rationale: no DB writes, no risk of
sample data leaking into real business state, and "Explore the app →" still lets the subscriber
enter the real app (empty states + Complete-profile banner guide them). If the user wants a true
in-app sandbox mode with sample Board/Hub/Calendar data, that is a follow-up item to confirm
with them first.
