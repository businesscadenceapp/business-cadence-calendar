# Business Cadence Calendar — Design Brainstorm

## Three Approaches

### Approach A: Executive Leather Planner
A rich, warm-toned design inspired by premium leather-bound executive planners. Deep mahogany and cream tones, serif typography, subtle paper texture. Feels like a high-end physical planner digitized.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Approach B: Clean Clinical Command Center ← CHOSEN
A crisp, structured dashboard aesthetic inspired by medical and operational command centers — fitting for a chiropractic practice owner. Navy, white, and sharp accent colors. Geometric, data-forward, authoritative. Feels like a tool built for serious operators who run multiple businesses.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Approach C: Warm Family Office
Soft, earthy tones with rounded corners and a cozy feel — designed to remind the couple that these are their family businesses. Terracotta, sage, and warm cream. Feels personal and grounding.
**Probability:** 0.02

---

## Chosen Approach: Clean Clinical Command Center

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets modern SaaS dashboard

**Core Principles:**
1. Information hierarchy is king — the most important meeting type is always visually dominant
2. Color is functional, not decorative — each business and meeting type has its own unambiguous color
3. Density with breathing room — pack useful information without crowding
4. Precision over decoration — no unnecessary ornamentation

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep navy `#0F1C2E` — authoritative, calm, professional
- Surface: Slightly lighter navy `#162236` — card backgrounds
- Chiropractic (anchor business): Emerald green `#10B981` — established, healthy, thriving
- CrossFit (growing): Amber/orange `#F59E0B` — energetic, on the rise
- Bubbles Realty (passive): Slate blue `#64748B` — steady, passive
- Daily Huddle: Soft lavender `#8B5CF6` — quick, light
- Weekly L10: Sky blue `#0EA5E9` — core operational
- Monthly Review: Teal `#14B8A6` — financial clarity
- Quarterly Offsite: Rose/coral `#F43F5E` — strategic, important

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width yearly calendar grid as the hero
- Left sidebar with legend and meeting type descriptions
- Month-by-month grid with color-coded event dots/bars
- Clicking a day or event opens a detail panel on the right

**Signature Elements:**
1. Color-coded meeting dots on calendar days — instantly scannable
2. A "Meeting Cadence Summary" strip above the calendar showing weekly/monthly/quarterly rhythm
3. Subtle grid lines on the calendar evoking graph paper / command center displays

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Click any day to see what meetings are scheduled
- Hover over a meeting type in the legend to highlight all instances on the calendar
- Toggle between "Year View" and "Month View"

**Animation:**
- Calendar months fade in with a stagger (30ms per month)
- Meeting detail panel slides in from the right (200ms ease-out)
- Hover on days: subtle scale(1.05) with 150ms ease-out

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Space Grotesk` — geometric, technical, modern
- Body: `Inter` — readable, neutral
- Monospace accents for dates/numbers: `JetBrains Mono`

**Brand Essence:**
"Your three businesses, one clear rhythm." — For driven couples who own multiple businesses and need structured clarity to protect both their productivity and their relationship.

**Brand Voice:**
Confident, direct, practical. No fluff.
- Headline example: "Own the Week. Own the Year."
- CTA example: "Your meeting rhythm, mapped."

**Signature Brand Color:** Deep navy `#0F1C2E`
