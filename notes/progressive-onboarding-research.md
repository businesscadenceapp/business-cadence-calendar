# Progressive Onboarding Research for TARSA

## Sources reviewed

- [Contextual Onboarding — UserOnboarding.Academy](https://useronboarding.academy/post/contextual-onboarding): Defines contextual onboarding as delivering a useful tip when it is needed; recommends dividing a long walkthrough into relevant action-based pieces and keeping tooltips concise.
- [How Notion Crafts a Personalized Onboarding Experience — Candu](https://www.candu.ai/blog/how-notion-crafts-a-personalized-onboarding-experience-6-lessons-to-guide-new-users): Describes Notion’s benefits-led, tailored start and progressive reveal of features as they become relevant to a user’s workflow.
- [Custom Onboarding — Asana Help](https://help.asana.com/s/article/custom-onboarding): Documents a configurable welcome screen, start screen, and selective onboarding path rather than forcing a single flow for all users.
- [Get Started with Todoist — Todoist Help](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/get-started-with-todoist-OgNNJR): Starts with the core action (create a project and first task), uses example task language, and adds more capabilities only after that foundation.

## TARSA decisions approved by the user

1. Replace the automatic 20-step feature tour with a three-card TARSA benefits overview.
2. Center the overview on automatic and manual Sleep Mode, private thought capture without interrupting a partner, and the two-hub business rhythm.
3. Keep a detailed tour as an optional five-stop "Explore TARSA" map in Settings.
4. Teach detailed workflows contextually when a person first creates a Task, Update, Issue, Goal, KPI, or Report.

## Approved overview copy themes

- Work stays at work: personal business hours automatically change the center sun to a moon; owners may manually toggle it at any time.
- Capture it—do not interrupt: store a business thought as a task, update, or issue instead of pulling a partner back into work at home.
- Run the business with rhythm: Command Center handles daily coordination; the Productivity Hub contains Goals, KPIs, Reports, Settings, and referrals.
